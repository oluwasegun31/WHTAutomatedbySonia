My story

There are certain emails that hit your inbox and you just close your eyes tight, “not again” you gently whisper. That was me. Not because I didn’t like the task that came with these emails, but I didn’t appreciate the process.

Every month, I spent hours copying vendor details from long Withholding schedules (WHT) into tax authority templates. The work was repetitive and time consuming. Thankfully, sometimes, I got help from colleagues.

But this process was more of copying and repetition.

And because withholding should be processed per vendor, one big schedule from the client must become many small files.

So, I built a tool to take care of the repetitive part for me.

Now, just like me, upload your WHT schedule and download a separate, ready-to-upload file for each vendor on REV360 or FCT-IRS.

Less copying. Less repetition. More time for what matters.
